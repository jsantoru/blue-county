Beverly reference area: NYS Geospatial Services / Orange County GIS.
Building footprints and selective public parcel geometry are independent NYS
reference sources; individual footprint source dates are retained. The 2025
NYS orthophoto is retained as an attributed research reference, not a game
texture. Source-specific notices remain applicable; these sources are not
represented as CC0 or USGS public-domain imagery.
https://gis.ny.gov/shareGIS/
https://gis.ny.gov/orthoimagery-faqs

Exact source metadata and usage notices: ../../docs/research-beverly/findings.md

Ordinary listing photographs are linked as observation references only; no
listing photograph or Google Street View imagery is distributed as a game asset.
